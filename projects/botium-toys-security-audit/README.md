# Botium Toys — Internal Security Audit Lab

## Overview

For this document-based audit of Botium Toys, a fictional retailer, I reviewed the supplied risk assessment and completed a controls and compliance checklist. Of 14 security controls, I marked 5 present and 9 absent or inadequate. My recommendations addressed excessive access, missing encryption, recovery planning, and monitoring gaps.

## Tools and Topics

- **Security controls:** Assessing access management, data protection, and physical safeguards.
- **Risk assessment:** Reviewing the scenario's assets and documented security gaps.
- **Compliance:** Reviewing checklist items covering PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC Type 1/Type 2 training categories.
- **Recommendations:** Documenting improvements to passwords, backups, intrusion detection, and legacy system maintenance.

## Workflow

1. **Reviewed the scenario.** Read the IT manager’s scope, goals, asset information, and risk assessment to understand Botium Toys’ operations and security concerns.
2. **Assessed the controls.** Compared the supplied evidence against 14 controls, including least privilege, separation of duties, encryption, backups, disaster recovery, password policies, and physical security.
3. **Completed the compliance checklist.** Reviewed 12 items grouped under PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC Type 1/Type 2 in the training worksheet.
4. **Identified gaps.** Flagged unrestricted employee access, unencrypted payment data, missing backups and recovery plans, weak password requirements, and absent intrusion detection.
5. **Documented recommendations.** Recommended tighter access controls, encryption, disaster recovery planning, stronger password policies and centralized password management, intrusion detection, and scheduled legacy system maintenance.

## Results

My checklist marked **5 controls present and 9 absent or inadequate**. The assessment was based on the scenario documents. The supplied report’s risk score of 8/10 was part of the exercise, not a score I calculated.
